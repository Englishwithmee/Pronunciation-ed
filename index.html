PART 1 · THEORY

How do we pronounce -ed?

Regular past simple verbs end in -ed, but we pronounce the ending in three different ways. Listen to the last sound of the base verb—not the last letter.

/t/

After voiceless sounds

/p/, /k/, /f/, /s/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/, /θ/

lookedwatchedworkedhelped

/d/

After voiced sounds

Vowels and voiced consonants, except /d/

climbedopenedtraveledplayed

/ɪd/

After /t/ or /d/

The -ed ending adds an extra syllable.

starteddecidedwantedneeded
Remember: watched = /t/, opened = /d/, decided = /ɪd/. The ending /ɪd/ makes an extra syllable; /t/ and /d/ do not. “-ed” is not always pronounced “ed”.

Listen to the textbook sentences

1. People started using smartphones. /ɪd/
2. They opened a door. /d/
3. Early cameras worked very slowly. /t/
